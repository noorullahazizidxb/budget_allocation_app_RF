DevMinds Budget App — free budget manager

DevMinds Budget App is a free budget manager: plan, allocate and track your budget, income and expenses in one simple app. Part of the DevMinds product family.

Features

  • Plan monthly budgets and allocate income to categories
  • Track expenses against each allocation
  • Clear reports on where your money goes
  • Light and dark themes, works in any browser
